Choosing the right package and saving electricity

When choosing an electricity package it helps to know how much energy you consume and to base the decision on your habits, for example whether most energy is used at night or during the day.

A correctly chosen package helps reduce energy costs, but reviewing and changing your consumption habits often brings an even bigger win.

With a fixed package the electricity price stays the same for the whole contract term, exchange fluctuations do not affect the bill, and costs are easier to plan.

We offer single- and dual-tariff pricing variants.

Single-tariff

  • With the single-tariff variant one agreed price applies to every period of the contract.
  • A single-tariff package suits you better if most of the electricity is consumed during the day.

Dual-tariff

With the dual-tariff variant day and night electricity consumption are metered separately.

  • A dual-tariff price can be chosen if the site has a meter that measures day and night consumption separately.
  • The day price applies Monday to Friday (except public holidays) from 07:00 to 22:00.
  • The night price applies Monday to Friday from 22:00 to 07:00 and all day on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays.

  • In a variable-price package the electricity price depends on the power exchange price.
  • A fixed sales margin is added on top of the exchange price.

For a long time variable pricing was considered cheaper than a fixed price, but recent experience has shown that the exchange price can just as well rise as fall, so one should be prepared for both scenarios.
