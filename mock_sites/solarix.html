Zero-margin renewable exchange package

Zero-margin renewable energy exchange package for private customers, where electricity is paid at the exchange price without any margin. In addition, a monthly fee applies, determined by your consumption over the last twelve months.

Frequently asked questions

What does the price consist of? The price consists of the electricity consumption cost according to the power exchange price and the Solarix monthly fee (based on the last 12 months of consumption: 3€, 4€, 6€ or 9€).

How is the package and the monthly fee determined? The package is determined by your annual electricity consumption.

Can I sell the surplus from my solar panels? Selling back happens at the exchange price with a service fee of 1 c/kWh (incl. VAT) added on top.
